Sprint 5 — Booking Lifecycle
Goal

Complete the operational lifecycle of a booking.

Backend Tasks
Cancel booking API.
Staff cancellation reason required.
Owner cancellation reason optional.

Reschedule booking API:
- booking must not be completed
- new slot must be free
- old slot becomes available
- transactions remain attached

Complete booking API:
- if remaining > 0, require confirmation
- system creates automatic cash transaction for remaining amount
- booking becomes Completed
- booking becomes locked

No-show API.

Hold expiry job:
- Hold expires after 12 hours
- Expired booking no longer blocks slot

Locked rules:
- completed bookings cannot be edited
- completed bookings cannot receive transactions
- completed bookings cannot be cancelled/rescheduled

Audit logs for all lifecycle changes.
Frontend Tasks

Critical Warnings

These warnings are very important:

Do not start with marketplace.
Do not build payment gateway now.
Do not skip audit logs.
Do not allow raw money edits.
Do not build desktop-first UI.
Do not overbuild pricing.
Do not ignore concurrency.

The project docs explicitly warn that mobile-first is mandatory because real users are staff on phones, and double booking is one of the most dangerous bugs.

I would add one more warning based on our frontend experience:

Do not confuse mobile-first with mobile-only.

Every frontend screen must be:

mobile-first
tablet-aware
desktop-usable
